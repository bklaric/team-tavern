Buttons

Filled ember once per screen. Card contact buttons are outlined.

Header

The logo leads home and New post is the header's one prominent button, outlined so it doesn't compete with the filled button of the page below it. Games opens the whole catalogue, the inbox and the bell carry their counts, and the account menu holds the rest. On a phone the logo is its mark, Games and notifications open full screen and the account menu as a sheet.

Notifications

The bell's list (brief 11.3), grouped by the player's own posts: a post that fits opens its own page, and a post of yours about to expire opens your posts, where it is renewed. Messages aren't here; the inbox has its own count in the header.

Facts and match marks

Everything a card shows about a post is a fact, Looking for included. Marks sit on the facts and say what fits the viewer's description and what doesn't; ≠ is information, not an error. A field the viewer filled in and the post left empty is a mismatch too. Below them is the group heading, which says how many the group is and how many more it wants.

A feed

Each fact is marked where it stands. A compared field the card doesn't otherwise show, such as a player's age, joins the end of the fact line.

Contact and messaging

Every card's contact button opens one panel: the contacts the owner shared and the conversation about the post. The owner's preference decides which comes first; a conversation already under way always does. On a desktop the panel is a side panel, on a phone the whole screen.

Your posts

The home page shows a player's own posts with their state and what they have produced. Renew is outlined once a post is in its last week or has expired; before that it is plain text.

Account

The account page (brief 11.5) is one definition list per section: the facts and contacts every post of the player's shows, edited here once for all of them, and what nobody else sees. A switch is on or off the moment it is pressed, where a checkbox waits for the form it sits in. The email row carries whether the address is confirmed, since until it is, the link confirming it is the only email the site sends.

Posting

The type chooser as posting's first step, and the controls of the post screen. A chosen pill takes the text color, not ember: it is an answer, not a call to act.
