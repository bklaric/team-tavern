## TL;DR draft (the In short box)

> **In short**
>
> - A Rocket League club holds up to 20 players, owner included, and you join only when its owner or a manager invites you. Nobody can apply, and the game has no club search.
> - Since the June 2025 update, owners and managers can invite anyone from the Recent Players menu, and clubmates show as online and can ask to join each other's parties.
> - You can be in one club at a time, and an owner can leave only after handing ownership to another member. An owner who leaves as the last member deletes the club and all its progress.
> - In the RLCS, a "club" is a team: three starters and up to one reserve, at least 13 years old, with two of the three citizens or legal residents of the team's region, or studying or working there. No rule ties it to an in-game club.
> - The 2027 season opens with the Club Kick-Off in London on 9–13 December, for 20 invited clubs and four open qualifier winners. From January, 64 teams play regional Club Leagues, and 40 of those places go through open Club Opens.

## Information gain

- **The 2025 club rules**, which no ranking page has: Recent Players invites, party requests, managers, medals, seasonal titles, and what deleting a club loses.
- **The 2027 RLCS from its rulebook**: the Opens' format (double elimination, Swiss, GSL, then an eight-team bracket), the roster (three starters, one reserve, coach and manager 18+), age 13+ with no Cabined Account, 2FA, one region per player, one roster addition per transfer window, and residency now counting study and work.
- **A changed rule**: 2026's residency rule was citizenship or permanent legal residency only. In 2027, in-person study or a work placement in the region counts too.
- **The disambiguation**: the in-game club and the RLCS club are unrelated, so a team needs no in-game club to enter.
- **Recruiting advice for each case**: filling a club with players who play at your hours, versus building a trio that's eligible for the Opens.

### H2: How Rocket League clubs work

Cover first, then:

- What a club is: a name, a tag, two colours, up to 20 members including the owner (Epic). It sits under Club on the main menu. Its members party up and queue as usual, and every queue is open to them (2018 post).
- Roles: owner, managers with the owner's powers except naming a new owner (Epic's banned-owner page), members.
- One club per account.
- Club matches: when every member of a team is in one club, its colours and name replace the team's, in Casual and Competitive, not in Private Matches or Tournaments (2018 post). If the colours are too close, one team takes its secondary colours or a neutral set.
- Medals and titles (v2.53, Epic): Goals, Saves and Assists, each with Prospect, Signature and Premier medals, reset every season. A title for the same tier in all three, and 12 titles on creation. Stats count only when at least two clubmates play in a party (Epic).
- Cross-platform membership (2018 post; Epic's friends list is cross-platform).
- Cabined Accounts may not have clubs (Epic's Clubs page).

### H2: Joining a club

- The only way in is an invite from the owner or a manager. You can't apply, and there's no search.
- How the invite arrives: a notification, then the friends list's Notifications, then Club Invites, where you accept (Epic's friends list page; AI Overview and videos agree). A player already in a club leaves it first.
- Since v2.53, owners and managers invite from Recent Players, which holds the last 25 players you played with or against (Epic's friends list page). A good random teammate can invite you straight after a match.
- Hedged: the 2018 rule was that you had to be in the owner's party to get an invite. Whether that still applies to friends is unconfirmed, so the guide says partying up is the sure way.

### H2: Making and running a club

- Create: Club on the main menu, Create Club, name, tag, colours, Accept (Epic).
- Rename and retag: owner only, once every 7 days (Epic). The game filters names and tags (2018 post).
- Colours missing: Color Blind Mode hides them (Epic). Players can also turn off club colours in Settings (2018 post).
- Ownership: transfer from a member's name on the Club screen (2018 post). Managers keep running the club if the owner can't. Epic Support can't change club roles, and a banned owner can't be replaced (Epic).
- Leaving: Club, then Leave Club (Epic). An owner transfers ownership first. An owner who is the only member deletes the club by leaving. Deleting loses medals, stats and titles; a manager or owner leaving doesn't (Epic, v2.53).
- Medals in match intros can be turned off in Club Customization (Epic).

### H2: RLCS clubs and the 2027 season

- Psyonix calls the 2027 season the Club Championship: three acts, the Club Kick-Off, the Club League with its Opens, and the Club Finale (rocketleague.com/competitive; X article).
- **The table**: the stages, with dates and team counts:
  - Club Kick-Off Opens: 5–22 Nov 2026; NA, EU, SAM, MENA; one winner each
  - Club Kick-Off: London, 9–13 Dec 2026; 24 teams, $2.5M
  - Club League Opens: TBA; seven regions; fill 40 places
  - Club League: from January 2027; 12 teams in NA and EU, 8 in MENA, SAM, OCE, APAC and SSA; single round robin, best of five
  - Club Finale: LAN, TBA; 16 teams
- Who's invited: 20 organisations, named by RLEsports (Gentle Mates, Heretics, …). Only invited teams earn Club Points (rulebook Attachment C, §2.6.1).
- Finale places by region (rulebook §2.2.8.3): EU and NA four each, MENA and SAM two, OCE, APAC and SSA one. The Kick-Off winner's region gets one more.
- No rule ties an RLCS team to an in-game club. The rulebook never mentions one.

### H2: Getting a team into the RLCS

- **The roster** (§3.8.2): three starters, up to one reserve, plus a coach and a manager, each at least 18. Nobody may be on two rosters.
- **Eligibility** (§3.1–3.7):
  - at least 13, or older where the law requires it
  - no Cabined Account, and 2FA on
  - an Epic account in your own name, in good standing, never bought or gifted
  - one account, one entry and one region for the whole event
  - parental consent under 18
- **Residency** (§3.8.3): two of three players, or three of four, must be citizens or residents of the region. Residency counts as long-term legal residency, in-person study or a work placement there. Countries are listed per region, and a few can choose (Colombia NA or SAM, Egypt and Morocco EU or MENA, Pakistan MENA or APAC). Coaches and managers don't count.
- **Registration** (§3.8.10): every player registers on start.gg's RLCS Club Championship hub. One creates the team and the others join by searching its name or by invite.
- **The Opens' format** (§2.2.2): double elimination down to 32 (best of three, then best of five from the last 192), a Swiss stage of two groups of 16, GSL groups, then an eight-team bracket played best of seven. Up to two days for the first stage, then weekends.
- **Roster changes** (§3.8.6): teams in a Club League get one transfer window and one addition, plus one exempt reserve.
- **Platforms and servers** (§2.2.10, §4.2.3): cross-platform. Early rounds run on the region's public servers (US-Central by default in NA), later rounds on RLCS servers.
- **What's still unannounced**, said once: Club League Open dates and sign-up, the Finale's place and date, whether 2027 has Majors or Worlds, and whether a team that qualifies keeps its place in 2028.

### H2: Finding a club, or a team

- **A club**: one whose members play your playlist, your region and your hours, since medals and stats come from playing together in parties. The official Discord's #club-recruitment channel (Epic's page). Recent Players after a good match.
- **Filling your club**: invite from Recent Players. Ask for players who play at your hours. Replace members who've stopped playing, since 20 is the cap.
- **An RLCS team**: two of three must be eligible in your region. Agree the region and who registers before the deadline. Pick a reserve who also meets the residency rule.
- **Post on TeamTavern**:
  - A player looking for a club or a team puts up a player post with their 2s and 3s rank, server and platform, ticks "Ranked", "Casual" or "Scrims and tournaments" under "Looking for", and says "looking for a club" in the description.
  - A club recruiting puts up a community post with its server, platform and "Looking for", and its name and tag in the description.
  - A trio or duo for the Opens puts up a group post with "RLCS" ticked under "Looking for" and its region.
  - Add your Epic ID as a contact.
