## Meta description

League's Solo/Duo takes duos within Riot's rank limits, with Grandmaster and Challenger solo only since July 2026. Flex takes parties of 1, 2, 3 or 5, and Ranked 5s five of any ranks.

For `Guides.purs`, which the other guides keep shorter: "Who can duo in League's Solo/Duo, tier by tier, what Flex allows and why four can't queue it, and Ranked 5s for a five of any ranks."

## TL;DR draft

> - Ranked Solo/Duo takes you alone or with one friend. From Iron to Platinum a duo can be about a tier apart: Iron and Bronze with up to Silver, Silver with up to Gold, Gold with Silver to Platinum, Platinum with Gold to Emerald.
> - From Emerald the window narrows: an Emerald reaches up to Diamond III, a Diamond goes by divisions, and Diamond I can duo with a Master under 200 LP.
> - Since July 2026, two Masters can duo within 400 LP of each other. Grandmaster and Challenger players can't duo at all.
> - Ranked Flex takes parties of 1, 2, 3 or 5 with no rank limits up to Diamond. A four can't queue it, and anyone partying with a Master or above must be Emerald or higher.
> - Ranked 5s takes a five of any ranks, on weekends only for now.
> - Ranked opens at level 30, with 20 champions and 10 Swiftplay or Normal Draft games.

### H2: Which queue takes your group

- **Answer first:** League is played five against five on Summoner's Rift. Ranked has two queues all year, and a third on weekends:
  - **Solo/Duo:** alone or with one friend, within the rank limits below. "Nobody you meet in Solo/Duo queue will be grouped with more than one other person" ([Riot](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/ranked-tiers-divisions-and-queues)).
  - **Flex:** a party of 1, 2, 3 or 5, never 4: "parties of 4 will have to stick to unranked matches". The reason, in Riot's words: a single player shouldn't be "the odd man out".
  - **Ranked 5s:** a full five of any ranks, Friday to Sunday, 4pm to 4am server time, with its own ladder and placements. Riot calls it an experiment; the current run ends 7 January 2027.
- Each queue has its own rank. Since 2026 a Flex rank starts from your Solo/Duo rank, though "Flex will never pull Solo up" ([/dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026)). One sentence.

### H2: Who can duo in Solo/Duo

- **Answer first:** the limit is set by tier, and it tightens as you climb. From Iron to Platinum a duo can be about a tier apart; from Emerald it narrows; in Diamond a partner "must be within two divisions"; Grandmaster and Challenger can't duo. Cite [Riot's Ranked Tiers page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/ranked-tiers-divisions-and-queues).
- **Table: who can duo with whom.** Columns: Your tier / You can duo with / For example. Riot's rows, word for word in the middle column, in the game's order:

  | Your tier | You can duo with | For example |
  |---|---|---|
  | Iron | Iron to Silver | Iron IV with Silver I: yes. Iron I with Gold IV: no |
  | Bronze | Iron to Silver | Bronze I with Silver I: yes. Bronze I with Gold IV: no |
  | Silver | Iron to Gold | Silver IV with Gold I: yes. Silver I with Platinum IV: no |
  | Gold | Silver to Platinum | Gold IV with Platinum I: yes. Gold I with Emerald IV: no |
  | Platinum | Gold to Emerald | Platinum IV with Emerald I: yes. Platinum I with Diamond IV: no |
  | Emerald | Platinum to Diamond III | Emerald I with Diamond IV: yes. Emerald IV with any Diamond: no |
  | Diamond IV to II | Emerald II to Diamond II | Diamond IV with Diamond II: yes. Diamond IV with Diamond I: no |
  | Diamond I | Diamond III to a Master under 200 LP | Diamond I with Master at 150 LP: yes. With Master at 250 LP: no |
  | Master | Diamond I if the Master has under 200 LP, or a Master within 400 LP | Masters at 50 and 350 LP: yes. At 0 and 450 LP: no |
  | Grandmaster | Nobody | |
  | Challenger | Nobody | |

  How the examples are chosen, for the writer: each "yes" fits both players' rows and is within two divisions wherever Diamond is involved; each "no" fails under every reading. The Master examples stay under 400 LP, since Grandmaster starts at 400 LP ([The Apex Tiers](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/master-grandmaster-and-challenger-the-apex-tiers)) and "Master players who are competing for Grandmaster are also restricted from playing duo", which Riot doesn't define. Pairs where the readings differ (Emerald II with Diamond III, Diamond II with Emerald II, Diamond I with Diamond III) aren't used as examples; see Not confirmed. Check every example against Riot's rows before writing.
- **Both players have to fit.** A duo is checked from both sides: Gold's row reaches up to Platinum, and Emerald's down to Platinum, so Gold and Emerald can't duo either way. Emerald's row reaches Diamond III, but Diamond's starts at Emerald II, so Emerald IV and III can't duo with a Diamond. Two or three sentences.
- **Hidden MMR can block a pair whose ranks fit.** Riot's page, as a short list: from Emerald to Diamond II, you can't queue with a player whose MMR is Master while you're Diamond or lower; Diamond I can't queue with a Master over 200 LP; and "Master players who are competing for Grandmaster are also restricted from playing duo". So two friends whose ranks fit can still be refused.
- **Korea is stricter:** "Players in KR cannot queue with Master or higher tiers." One sentence. Riot's page names no other region; don't repeat the China claims.
- **A promotion can end a duo.** Ranks move every game, so a duo at the edge of a row won't fit after one of them climbs a division. One sentence, stated as what follows from the table.

### H2: Master, Grandmaster and Challenger

- **Answer first:** since [patch 26.15](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes) (29 July 2026), Masters can duo "only with players close to them in rank": a Diamond I if the Master has under 200 LP, or another Master within 400 LP. "GM, Challenger, and players with GM or Challenger MMR will not be allowed to duo."
- **Why GM and Challenger are solo:** Riot's reason, quoted once: "smaller player pools and extremes of skill give duos an outsized impact on game outcomes". Riot says it will "keep looking for ways to bring duo queue back to GM and Challenger".
- **The 2026 history,** three bullets: duo at every rank in most regions from [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/) (January); turned off for Master and up where the April reset ran ([26.9](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes)); back for Master in July. So a page that says "Master can't duo" or "duo at every rank" is out of date. No names.
- **A Grandmaster can still play with friends** in Flex, with any party of Emerald or above, by Riot's [Apex Tiers page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/master-grandmaster-and-challenger-the-apex-tiers): "You cannot, however, queue with any player ranked below Emerald", and in Ranked 5s with anyone.
- Short H2: about 200 words.

### H2: Flex and Ranked 5s

- **Flex. Answer first:** a party of 1, 2, 3 or 5, and "no Flex rank restrictions for Diamond and below". With a Master or above in the party, everyone must be Emerald or higher. Cite Riot's Ranked Tiers page.
  - **Four can't queue.** A four adds a fifth, splits into a duo and a pair, or plays Normal Draft or Swiftplay.
  - **A wide party pays in lobbies.** Riot: parties "with large rank disparities (e.g., an Iron player grouping with a Diamond player) may result in more difficult matchups". One sentence.
  - **A full five loses Aegis of Valor:** "playing with a full premade group in Ranked Flex will not apply Aegis of Valor" ([Matchmaking page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/matchmaking)). Aegis now only doubles LP on a win (26.15). Writer's call whether it earns a sentence.
- **Ranked 5s. Answer first:** a five of any ranks, "From Unranked to Challenger", playing other fives ([Riot's Ranked 5s page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/ranked-5s)).
  - Friday to Sunday, 4pm to 4am server time, widened in [26.20](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/) (October 2026). Server time, not the reader's, so no `<time>` conversion.
  - Its own five placements and ladder, no decay, and it doesn't count toward the season's Victorious rewards.
  - It's Riot's second experiment, running to 7 January 2027. Say "for now", and recheck then (see Keeping it current).
- **What to do,** three bullets: a duo inside the table plays Solo/Duo; a mixed group of three or five up to Diamond plays Flex; a five that includes a Grandmaster and a Gold plays Ranked 5s on the weekend.

### FAQ (6)

1. **What ranks can duo in League of Legends?** Up to Platinum, about a tier apart: Iron and Bronze with up to Silver, Silver with up to Gold, Gold with Silver to Platinum, Platinum with Gold to Emerald. Emerald reaches up to Diamond III, Diamond goes by divisions, Diamond I can duo with a Master under 200 LP, and Masters within 400 LP of each other. Grandmaster and Challenger can't duo.
2. **Can Gold duo with Emerald?** No. Gold reaches only Platinum in Solo/Duo. A Gold and an Emerald can play Flex together, or Ranked 5s.
3. **Can Diamond and Master duo?** Only a Diamond I with a Master under 200 LP. Since July 2026, Masters can also duo with each other within 400 LP.
4. **Can Grandmaster or Challenger duo?** Not in Solo/Duo, since patch 26.15 in July 2026. They can play Flex with friends of Emerald and above, and Ranked 5s with anyone.
5. **Can you play Flex with 4 people?** No. Flex takes parties of 1, 2, 3 or 5. A four adds a fifth, splits, or plays Normal Draft or Swiftplay.
6. **What do you need to play ranked in League?** Level 30, at least 20 champions, and 10 Swiftplay or Normal Draft games. Then five placement matches in each ranked queue.
