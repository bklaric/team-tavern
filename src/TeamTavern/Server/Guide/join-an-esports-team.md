Almost every esports team you can join is an amateur team playing its game's own team tournament, such as Valorant Premier or League of Legends Clash, or a league, and scrims against other teams.

## Your game's team format

| Game | Format | Team size |
|---|---|---|
| Valorant | Premier | 5 to 7 |
| League of Legends | Clash | 5 |
| Apex Legends | ALGS Challenger Circuit | 3 |

### Valorant

You create or join a team in the Premier tab, and a team has five to seven players. See [Riot's guide to Premier teams](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-team-creation-management).

[Valorant players and groups on TeamTavern](/games/valorant)

## Find a team

Put up a player post that says your rank, role, region, languages and the hours you're online. Then message the group posts that fit you.
