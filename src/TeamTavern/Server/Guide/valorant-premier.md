Valorant Premier is a weekly league in the client for a team of five to seven players, on PC. A Stage runs about seven weeks, and each week a team plays up to two matches in one-hour evening windows on its zone's own clock: in Stage V26A6, on Thursday, Saturday and in most zones Sunday, mostly at 19:00. From Open to Elite, every team with 600 points goes to the playoffs, and since a win is worth 100 points and a loss 25, a team that plays every match needs only four wins. Losses earn points too, so a team that plays every week can lose ten of fourteen and still qualify, while every missed week takes away matches that would have earned them.

This guide is for a player with a team, or part of one, who wants to play Premier with people they chose, or to join a team that does. It covers when Premier runs, how a team's division is set, Premier Score and the playoffs, what each player needs and the roster rules, and how to find the players you're missing. For other games, see [every game's team format](/guides/join-an-esports-team#your-games-team-format).

> **In short**
>
> - Premier is Valorant's in-client league for a team of five to seven players, on PC only. A team plays up to two matches a week for about seven weeks, then a playoff for the teams that qualify.
> - In Stage V26A6, which runs from 21 October to 5 December 2026 with the playoffs on 6 December, weekly matches are played in one-hour windows on Thursday, Saturday and, in most zones, Sunday, between 19:00 and 21:00 on the zone's own clock.
> - From Open to Elite, a win is worth 100 Premier Score and a loss 25, and 600 qualifies for the playoffs in a seven-week Stage. A team that plays both matches every week needs only four wins.
> - A team's division comes from its top five players' Competitive MMR, weighted toward the best player when their skill differs widely. After the team's first match of the Stage, the division and zone are locked, and nobody can join whose MMR would move the division.
> - Every player needs SMS verification, ranked placements completed at least once, and no current ban or restriction. Below the Contender division there's no rank minimum.
> - From Open to Elite, the playoffs are single-elimination brackets played in one evening, from a 15-minute queue window, and the five who queue for the first match play every match.

## When Premier runs

<img class="guide-cover" src="/images/games/400/valorant.webp" alt="" width="400" height="600" loading="lazy"> Premier runs in Stages, and a Stage "roughly coincides with an Act", by [Riot's page on joining a team](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-joining-leaving-teams). A team plays "Up to 2 matches per week, for 7 consecutive weeks", then the playoffs. Each week has one map, and the week resets on Tuesday. Riot has announced recent Stages' weeks on its X account, and its [post for Stage V26A6](https://x.com/VALORANT/status/2110400239605895330) has seven weeks of matches from 21 October to 5 December 2026, and the playoffs on Sunday 6 December.

**The time of day** depends on your zone. Riot's [Queues & Matchmaking page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-queues-matchmaking) says "Most Weekly matches occur around 7 PM local time, but this can vary between 7-9 PM", and sends players to the Premier tab for their own. Riot's schedule for V26A6, as [tracker.gg's Premier pages](https://tracker.gg/valorant/premier) show it, has these weekly match windows from Open to Elite in each zone, and these playoff queue times:

| Zone | Weekly matches | Playoffs, 6 December |
|---|---|---|
| **US East, US West** | Thursday 19:00, Saturday 20:00, Sunday 19:00 | 19:00 |
| **Brazil, Latin America North** | Thursday 19:00, Saturday 20:00, Sunday 19:00 | 19:00 |
| **Latin America South** | Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 21:00 | 20:00 |
| **Northern Europe, Europe DACH, Europe France, Eastern Europe, Türkiye** | Thursday 19:00, Saturday 20:00, Sunday 19:00 | 19:00 |
| **Europe IBIT** | Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 19:00 | 19:00 |
| **Middle East** | Thursday and Saturday 19:00 | 19:00 |
| **Asia, Korea** | Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 20:00 | 20:00 |
| **Japan** | Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 21:00 | 20:00 |
| **Oceania** | Thursday 19:00, Saturday 20:00 | 19:00 |
| **South Asia** | Thursday and Saturday 20:30 | 20:00 |

**Every time is on the zone's own clock**: Eastern Time for US East, Pacific Time for US West, UK time for Northern Europe and Central European Time for the other European zones, UTC+3 for Türkiye and the Middle East, UTC+8 for Asia, UTC+9 for Japan and Korea, India's time for South Asia, UTC+11 for Oceania, UTC−3 for Brazil and Latin America South, and UTC−6 for Latin America North. The windows kept their hour when the clocks went back in Europe and North America, so a player on the zone's clock queues at the same time all Stage. A player whose own clock differs from their zone's converts from the zone's.

**A weekly window lasts an hour.** A team can play both of its matches in one window if the first finishes in time, and then has no more that week: by Riot's [Weekly Matches page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-weekly-matches), "If you play two matches on Thursday on Bind, you won't be able to play on Saturday." Queue early in the window: Riot expects a match within eight minutes, and a team that queues near the end may not find one. Practice matches have windows of their own, which don't count toward Premier Score or the two-a-week limit. By Riot's [wiki](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Premier), though, a practice match locks the team's division like any other.

**Contender and Invite**, the top two divisions, generally play both weekly matches on one evening, from a 15-minute queue window that matches teams when it closes, by Riot's [page on those divisions](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-contender-invite-divisions). A loss there earns no points, and teams qualify for the playoffs by their place in the standings.

**Not every Stage is seven weeks.** In 2026, [patch 12.05](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-12-05/) gave Stage V26A2 six weeks of matches, with 500 points to reach the playoffs, and [patch 13.04](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-13-04/) gave V26A5 five weeks, with 450. The times above are V26A6's, and the Premier tab shows each Stage's.

## Divisions, and why the first match matters

From the top, Premier's [divisions](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-divisions) are Invite, Contender, Elite 1–5, Advanced 1–5, Intermediate 1–5 and Open 1–5. A team only ever plays teams in its own division and zone.

**The division comes from the top five players.** As soon as a team is made, it gets a provisional division from the average Competitive MMR of its five best players. Where their skill differs a lot, "the division is adjusted to weigh the best player's MMR more heavily." So a team of Golds with one Immortal friend lands higher than its average alone would put it, and the Golds play stronger teams. The provisional division changes with every roster or zone change until the team plays its first match of the Stage.

**The first match locks the team.** After it, the division and zone are fixed for the Stage. By Riot's [team rules](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-team-creation-management), "Team Owners/Captains cannot invite players who would move the team's current division", and Riot warns: "If you remove a teammate, there is no guarantee that you can re-add them." So fill the roster and pick the zone before the team's first match, practice matches included.

**Promotion comes from the playoffs.** From Open to Elite, a team moves up only by winning its division's playoff bracket. The promotion is at least one division, and only for the next Stage, so a team that skips that Stage loses it. Below Invite, "Teams are not demoted based on performance", though a weaker roster can start the next Stage lower.

**Contender and Invite** take only players with Contender Eligibility, which a player has if, in the current or previous Stage, they played a match in Contender or Invite, reached a peak of Immortal 3 or higher in Competitive, or played a playoff match in Elite 5. The top teams of each Invite division can earn a place in a Challengers league, which [joining a Premier team](/guides/join-an-esports-team#valorant-premier) covers.

A five that also wants to queue Competitive between matches can play as a five-stack of any ranks, which [Valorant team size, party size and playing ranked with friends](/guides/valorant-ranked-with-friends) covers.

## Premier Score and the playoffs

**Points.** From Open to Elite, a weekly win is worth 100 Premier Score and a loss 25, by Riot's [Team Hub & Score page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-team-hub-score). Playoff matches give no points.

**600 points reach the playoffs** from Open to Elite, by Riot's [playoff rules](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-playoff-tournaments), however many other teams make it. Higher scores get better seeding. In a seven-week Stage, a team that plays both matches every week plays 14:

- **Four wins and ten losses** make 650, enough.
- **Three wins and eleven losses** make 575, short.
- **A team that starts late** has fewer matches to earn them in. With four weeks left it needs six wins from eight, with three weeks left it has to win all six, and with two weeks left it can't reach 600. As [Riot's page on joining a team](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-joining-leaving-teams) puts it, "If you join too late, however, there may not be enough match opportunities for you to earn a high enough Premier Score to qualify for playoffs."

So start in the first week, and play both matches every week.

**Playoff night.** A division's qualified teams are split into single-elimination brackets of up to eight teams, and a bracket "may start with as few as 2 depending on who queues up at the designated time". The queue window is 15 minutes, by Riot's [wiki](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Premier), not an hour, and "If your team misses the tournament queue window, you will not be able to participate." From Open to Elite, the bracket is played straight through in one evening, with at least five minutes between matches, and each match is best of one, with a map pick and ban that the party leader locks in for the team.

**No subs below Contender.** "The 5 players that queue up for the first match of the tournament will be locked in for the rest of the matches in that tournament." Contender and Invite play their playoffs over more than one day, and can swap players from the roster between rounds.

**What winning gives.** Winning the bracket earns promotion, a title and a gun buddy, and the Champion's Aura on your player card until the end of the next Stage. Everyone who plays at least one match gets the Stage's player card. Riot's [rewards page](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/rewards/premier-rewards-perks) lists the rest.

## What each player needs, and the roster

By [Riot's page on joining a team](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-joining-leaving-teams), every player needs:

- **SMS verification**, kept for the whole Stage.
- **Ranked placements, once.** "You must have completed at least one ranked placement in your account's lifetime." Competitive opens at account level 20, as [Valorant team size, party size and playing ranked with friends](/guides/valorant-ranked-with-friends) covers, so a new account reaches level 20 and plays its placements before it can join.
- **No current ban or restriction**, which "includes AFK restrictions, even if those AFKs were unintentional." A ban or restriction during a Stage disqualifies the player for the rest of it. They stay on the roster, but they can't play.

There's no rank requirement below Contender, and no Premier on console: Riot's [wiki](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Premier) says Premier is "available only on PC".

**The roster.** A team has five to seven players, and a player can be on one team at a time. By [Riot's team rules](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/valorant/gameplay/premier-team-creation-management), the player who creates the team is its owner, and can name one captain. Both can invite and remove players. The name, of 5 to 15 characters, and the tag, of 1 to 5, can't be changed once the Create Team window closes. The owner doesn't have to play: the party leader queues the team and locks in its map picks and bans.

**Two matches a week, for each player.** "Both teams and individuals are limited to 2 matches per week," so a player who plays two matches for one team and then switches can't play for the new one until the week resets. Any five from the roster can play a weekly match.

**The team tag** shows beside a player's Riot ID once they've played two weekly matches or one playoff match for the team.

## Finding a team for Premier

Recruit for the whole Stage: players in your zone who can make its evenings every week, at a rank near the team's, in the roles you're short of.

**Before the first match.** Fill the roster before the team plays, or join a team that hasn't played yet this Stage.

**Six or seven, not five.** A team of five can't play a week in which one of the five can't make any of its windows, and every missed week costs points. A sixth or seventh player keeps the weekly matches going. For playoff night, be sure of your five, since below Contender nobody can be swapped in.

**Rank.** Recruit near the team's top five. A much stronger player lifts the whole team into a harder division, and after the first match, a player whose MMR would move the division, usually a much stronger one, can't join.

**Roles.** A five usually wants a Duelist, an Initiator, a Controller and a Sentinel, with the fifth on whatever the map needs. Say which roles you're short of, and whether you need someone to make the calls.

**Keep a good team.** A team's history and Premier MMR carry over from Stage to Stage, by Riot's [wiki](https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Premier), and its score starts again at zero. A team that worked is worth keeping for the next Stage. [How to make an esports team](/guides/make-an-esports-team) covers tryouts and keeping a roster together.

**Post on TeamTavern.** Put up a player post with your "Rank", your "Role", "In-game leader" if you make the calls, "PC" under "Platform" and the hours you're online, tick "Premier" under "Looking for", and add your Riot ID and Discord. A team short of players puts up a group post with a rank range around its top five, the roles it's missing, how many players it wants, and its zone and evenings in the description. Then filter the posts for "Premier" and message the ones that fit.

## Frequently asked questions

### What time is Valorant Premier?

In the evening, on your zone's own clock, in one-hour windows. Riot says most weekly matches are around 19:00, varying between 19:00 and 21:00. In Stage V26A6, from Open to Elite, the windows open at 19:00 or 20:00 in most zones, 20:00 in Asia and Korea, 20:30 in South Asia, and 21:00 in Japan and Latin America South. The Premier tab shows your zone's exact times.

### What days is Valorant Premier on?

In Stage V26A6, from Open to Elite, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday in most zones, and Thursday and Saturday in Oceania, South Asia and the Middle East. A team plays up to two matches a week, so it needn't play every evening. The playoffs are on 6 December 2026, the Sunday after the last week.

### How many matches do you play in Premier?

Up to two a week, for each team and each player. From Open to Elite, a seven-week Stage gives a team 14 matches, then the playoffs for teams with enough points.

### How many points do you need for Premier playoffs?

From Open to Elite, 600 Premier Score in a seven-week Stage. A win is worth 100 and a loss 25, so a team that plays all 14 matches qualifies with four wins. Shorter Stages have had lower bars: in 2026, 500 for a six-week Stage and 450 for a five-week one. Contender and Invite teams qualify by their place in the standings.

### How many players are on a Premier team?

Five to seven. Any five from the roster play a weekly match, and each player can play two matches a week.

### Is there a rank requirement for Premier?

Not below the Contender division. Every player needs SMS verification and ranked placements completed at least once. A team's division comes from its top five players' MMR, weighted toward the best player when their skill differs widely. Contender and Invite take only players with Contender Eligibility, such as a peak of Immortal 3 or higher in the current or previous Stage.

### Can you play Premier on console?

No. Premier is PC only.

### When are the Premier playoffs?

At the end of each Stage. In Stage V26A6, the playoffs are on Sunday 6 December 2026, by Riot's schedule. From Open to Elite, each bracket is played that evening from a 15-minute queue window at 19:00 or 20:00 on the zone's clock, and a team that misses the window is out. Contender and Invite play over more than one day.

### Can you join a Premier team mid-Stage?

Yes, at any point, but after the team's first match of the Stage, only if your MMR wouldn't move its division. A team that starts with two weeks or less left can't reach 600 points.

## Find your team

- Make the team and pick the zone before its first match.
- Recruit six or seven, so one player's missed week doesn't cost the team its matches.
- Play both matches every week: four wins in fourteen reach 600.
- Bring five sure players for playoff night, since below Contender nobody can be swapped in.
