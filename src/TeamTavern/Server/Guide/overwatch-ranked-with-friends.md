Overwatch lets friends of any rank queue Competitive's Role Queue together, but not on equal terms. A group whose ranks are close is a narrow group and plays like anyone else. A group whose ranks are far apart is a wide group: it waits longer, gains and loses less rank, and as a group of four it can't queue at all. The limits for the top ranks changed in August 2026 without a patch note, and the answers that rank highest still give older ones.

This guide is for a player who plays Competitive, the game's ranked mode, with friends, or wants a group to play it with. It covers how big a group can be, which ranks make a narrow group, what a wide group costs, which of your three role ranks counts, friends without a rank this season, PC and console, and how to find a group in range. For teams and tournaments, see [every game's team format](/guides/join-an-esports-team#your-games-team-format).

> **In short**
>
> - Competitive's Role Queue is 5v5: one Tank, two DPS and two Support. A group can be any size up to five, and the game fills the rest. 6v6 Open Queue takes up to six.
> - A group is narrow when everyone is close in rank: within five divisions from Bronze to Diamond, four in Master, three in Grandmaster and two in Champion. Five divisions is the same division one rank away, so a Gold 3 makes a narrow group with anyone from Silver 3 to Platinum 3.
> - A group further apart still queues Role Queue, as a wide group: it plays only other wide groups, waits longer and gains and loses less rank. A wide group of four can't queue at all.
> - The rank that counts is your rank in the role you queue, so a group can often stay narrow by picking roles.
> - A friend without a rank in a role this season plays one Competitive match in it alone before grouping with ranked friends. Competitive opens after 50 wins.

## Team size and party size

<img class="guide-cover" src="/images/games/400/overwatch.webp" alt="" width="400" height="600" loading="lazy"> Overwatch, called Overwatch 2 until February 2026, has two Competitive queues:

- **Role Queue** is five against five, with one Tank, two DPS (the game says Damage) and two Support on each team. A group can be one to five players, and the game fills the empty places.
- **6v6 Open Queue** is six against six, with any heroes. A group can be up to six.

Every group size can queue Role Queue, with one exception: a wide group of four. A narrow group of four queues like any other, so answers that say four-stacks can't play Competitive have it wrong. The [patch notes that brought in wide groups](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/04/) give the reason: "Wide Groups of four players may not queue", because "solo players are never required to make a Wide match", and a wide four would need a solo player to fill its fifth place.

Competitive opens after 50 wins, raised from 20 by the [May 2026 patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/05/), so a friend new to the game plays Quick Play until then. Quick Play has no rank limits, and Stadium Ranked, which keeps its own rank, has had none since the [June 2026 patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/06/) removed them.

## Which ranks make a narrow group

From Bronze to Diamond, a group is narrow when everyone is within five divisions of each other. Every rank has five divisions, 5 up to 1, so five divisions apart is the same division one rank away: a Gold 3 makes a narrow group with anyone from Silver 3 to Platinum 3. The [patch notes that brought in wide groups](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/04/) set the rule: players "must be within five divisions of each other player in their group to be in a Narrow Group". Above Diamond the limits are tighter, and the game's Competitive panel gives them: in Master, "Wide Groups are more than 4 Divisions apart", in Grandmaster more than 3, and in Champion more than 2.

| Your rank | Narrow with players within | For example |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze to Diamond**, Emerald included | 5 divisions: the same division one rank up or down | Gold 3: Silver 3 to Platinum 3 |
| **Master** | 4 divisions | Master 5: Diamond 4 to Master 1 |
| **Grandmaster** | 3 divisions | Grandmaster 4: Master 2 to Grandmaster 1 |
| **Champion** | 2 divisions | Champion 3: Champion 5 to Champion 1 |

A group is held to its widest gap: its highest and lowest players must fit, not only each pair of neighbours. Between two ranks with different limits, such as Diamond and Master, Blizzard doesn't say whose limit applies, so count on the tighter one: a Diamond 3 is sure to be narrow with a Master only up to Master 4.

**Two ranks apart is never narrow in a group of three.** Emerald, added in the [August 2026 patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/08/), sits between Platinum and Diamond, and the panel counts it with the ranks around it. So Platinum 1 and Diamond 5 are six divisions apart, and a Platinum and a Diamond player can no longer be narrow in a group of three or more, and neither can a Gold and an Emerald player, or a Silver and a Platinum one. Friends who were narrow before August may be wide now.

**Leave room at the edge.** Blizzard's notes say within five, but the panel says groups "may be Wide when 5 Divisions apart", so a group exactly five apart may already be wide, and ranks move after every match. Below Master, a group whose members share one rank has room to drift.

**A third player can tip it.** Players find that two friends can be a little further apart than a group of three or more, so a third friend whose rank is between theirs can turn a narrow duo wide. Check the queue again whenever someone joins.

Some guides still say any Grandmaster or Champion makes a group wide, or give Master and Grandmaster two divisions and Champion one. Those were the rules of April 2024 and December 2025. The panel has given the limits in the table since Season 4 began in August 2026, and it adds that "Maximum width is subject to change".

## Wide groups: what they cost

A group too far apart to be narrow still queues Role Queue, as a wide group, but it pays for it:

- **It plays only wide groups.** "Solo players will only play with other solo players or 'Narrow Groups'", as Blizzard's [Season 10 matchmaking post](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24061006/defense-matrix-update-season-10-and-beyond/) puts it, so a wide group waits for another wide group.
- **It waits longer, and its rank moves less.** The [patch notes that brought in wide groups](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/04/) say wide groups "have increased queue times", and "the wider the group is, the less their ranks will change when winning or losing".
- **It earns no Challenger Score**, by the [December 2025 patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/12/), so a wide match does nothing for the Top 500 leaderboard.
- **A wide group of four can't queue.**

The game tells you before you queue. A "Wide Match" badge appears under the card of the role that would make the group wide, and a role that can't be queued is locked.

6v6 Open Queue has no wide groups at all, by the [March 2025 patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/03/): a group has to be narrow to queue it. When Blizzard's community team [set its limits that month](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/grouping-up-in-6v6-open-queue-competitive-play/957097), they were wider than Role Queue's: players from Bronze to Diamond had to be within ten divisions, Master and Grandmaster within five, and Champion within three. Blizzard hasn't restated them since.

A group that wants full rank gains stays narrow: it picks roles that fit, which the next section covers, or splits into a narrow group and a solo or duo. A group that plays for the fun of it can accept wide.

## Role ranks and friends without a rank

Each player has a rank for Tank, for DPS and for Support, as the panel says: "Players have different Skill Tiers for each hero role". The one that counts for the group is the rank for the role you queue. A Gold 2 Support whose DPS is Diamond makes a narrow group with Gold friends as Support, not as DPS.

That makes roles the first thing to try when a group comes up wide. Role ranks are often far apart: of the Overwatch player posts on TeamTavern that give two or more role ranks, 395 of 1,655 have them two or more ranks apart (TeamTavern's posts, 2021–2026, counted 21 October 2026). A player like that is narrow with a friend in one role and wide in another. Before a session, compare role ranks and pick the roles that keep everyone inside the table's limits.

A friend without a rank in a role this season can't group with ranked players until they've played one Competitive match in it alone. After that they have a predicted rank, which counts for the group and moves with every match, so a pair that fits after one match may not after the next. A rank takes 10 placement matches in each role, by the [August 2025 patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/08/).

Every three seasons all ranks go back to Unranked, as they did with Season 1 in February 2026 ([patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/02/)), so at the start of those seasons everyone plays a match alone in each role before the group can play together.

## PC and console

Competitive splits players by how they play, not by platform. It has two pools, which the [August 2025 patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/08/) renamed: the Mouse and Keyboard pool, which every PC player is in, and the Controller pool, for console players on a controller. "To play on the Controller pool, you must play on a console platform."

- **Each pool keeps its own rank.** A console player who switches to the Mouse and Keyboard pool in Gameplay Options plays with PC players, on a rank Blizzard says "will be separate from your statistics in the Controller Pool". So they place again, and the gap with PC friends is measured on that rank.
- **Quick Play takes everyone.** Blizzard doesn't say how a group with a PC player and a console player on a controller is matched in Competitive, so a group that doesn't want to move anyone to mouse and keyboard can always play Quick Play together.

## Finding a group in range

Recruit players within five divisions of everyone already in the group, fewer from Master up, in the roles you queue, in the same pool, at the hours you play.

**Range first.** Use the table, and leave room: a group with a division or two to spare keeps fitting as ranks move, and one that's exactly at the limit doesn't.

**A four has to stay narrow.** A narrow four queues like any other, but a four that turns wide can't queue at all, so a group of four near the limit is better off as three, or with a fifth.

**Roles.** A full five needs one Tank, two DPS and two Support. Name the roles you're short of, and the rank you have in each.

**Keep a good teammate.** After a good match with a stranger, add them by their BattleTag.

**From ranked to a team.** A group that plays every week and wants more can enter FACEIT's leagues, which [joining an Overwatch team](/guides/join-an-esports-team#overwatch) covers, and [How to make an esports team](/guides/make-an-esports-team) covers keeping a team together.

**Post on TeamTavern.** Ranked is what most Overwatch players on TeamTavern have been after since 2021: 2,378 of the 2,820 player posts tick it. Put up a player post with your rank in each role you play and your platform, and tick "Ranked" under "Looking for". A group looking for players puts up a group post with a rank range the whole group can stay narrow with, inside the table's limits from every member. That's narrower than many Overwatch group posts so far: 63 of the 208 that give a rank range ask, in at least one role, for players across two ranks or more, such as Gold 5 to Platinum 1, whose ends can't be narrow together in a group of three (TeamTavern's posts, 2021–2026, counted 21 October 2026). Then filter the posts by rank, role and platform and message the ones that fit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Overwatch 5v5 or 6v6?

Both. Competitive's Role Queue is five against five: one Tank, two DPS and two Support. 6v6 Open Queue is six against six, with any heroes.

### Can you play Competitive as a group of four?

Yes, if the group is narrow. Only a wide group of four can't queue, because its fifth place would need a solo player in a wide match.

### What ranks can play together in Overwatch?

Any ranks can queue Role Queue together, but only players within five divisions of each other, the same division one rank up or down, make a narrow group from Bronze to Diamond. Master allows four divisions, Grandmaster three and Champion two. Further apart, the group is wide.

### What is a wide group in Overwatch?

A group whose ranks are too far apart to be narrow. It plays only other wide groups, waits longer, and gains and loses less rank. A wide group of four can't queue, and 6v6 Open Queue takes no wide groups.

### Can you play ranked with a friend who has no rank?

Yes, once they've played one Competitive match in that role alone. After that their predicted rank counts for the group.

### Can PC and console players play ranked together?

A console player who switches to mouse and keyboard plays in the Mouse and Keyboard pool, with PC players, on a rank of its own. Blizzard doesn't say what happens when a PC player groups with a console player on a controller, so the sure ways are for the console player to switch, or for the group to play Quick Play together.

## Find your group

- Keep the group inside the table's limits, with room to spare, and remember that a group of three or more is never narrow across two ranks.
- Pick roles to fit: the rank that counts is the one for the role you queue.
- Keep a four narrow, or play as three or five.
- Have a friend without a rank play one match alone in each role they'll queue.
